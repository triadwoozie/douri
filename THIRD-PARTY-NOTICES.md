## Mutable Instruments Beads (granular engine of Pro Wave Texture)

Pro Wave Texture (`crates/prowave-engine/src/native_fx/texture/`) is a Rust port of the
granular engine of the Beads texture synthesizer: grain scheduling, the recording buffer and
its freeze, the delay mode, the quality modes, the feedback path and the reverb. Source:
https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack, `beads/dsp` at commit
f4cfa7697095a28d292bff758beb51deb49b5a86, and `stmlib` at commit
e3bd7c9cc00e4364166f9905c0509b6ffd0535ec. The test
`crates/prowave-engine/tests/texture_parity.rs` builds the original C++ (a copy of the files
it needs is in `crates/prowave-engine/tests/texture_reference/eurorack/`, with only the sine
table kept from `beads/resources.cc`) and checks the port against it.
